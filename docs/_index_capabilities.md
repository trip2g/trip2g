---

- **[Publish from Obsidian](/en/user/two_way_sync)** — your vault stays on your machine; sync pushes notes live, one-way or two-way.
- **[Publish to Telegram](/en/user/telegram)** — write once in Obsidian, post to your channel on a schedule, links stay intact.
- **[Charge for content](/en/user/monetization)** — leave `free: true` off and a note is subscriber-only; take payment via crypto, Patreon, or Boosty.
- **[An AI that answers from your notes](/en/user/mcp)** — connect any MCP client and readers query your knowledge base directly.
- **[Make it look how you want](/en/user/templates)** — compose pages from frontmatter widgets, or drop in your own Jet templates.
- **[Run it yourself](/en/user/selfhosted)** — one Go process on SQLite; `docker compose up` on a laptop, a VM, or Fly.
