A recorder such as Krisp captures the call, and an LLM pipeline turns it into linked notes: the verbatim transcript, a call note whose topics quote it, concept notes for people and terms, a daily note with action checkboxes. Not a summary plus a chat bot, but a graph that grows with every call. Next to it, agents keep the team's journal and report to the team on their own.
