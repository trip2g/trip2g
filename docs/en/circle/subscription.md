Like Boosty or Patreon, except the reader pays for a living knowledge base, not a feed of posts. Part of the vault is open to everyone, the rest to subscribers who pay through Patreon, Boosty, a paid Telegram group or crypto. A subscriber's AI agent connects to the same base over MCP in subscription-only mode: instead of transcribing hundreds of hours of an expert's videos, you plug their always-current base into your agent.
