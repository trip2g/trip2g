# Ignatius of Loyola — Spiritual Exercises

The full text of the **Spiritual Exercises** (*Ejercicios espirituales*,
1522–1548) of Ignatius of Loyola, the Spanish priest who founded the Jesuit order.
Not a treatise but a method: a structured guide for a 30-day retreat that orders
one's life through discernment of inner movements and free choice.

Contains the full corpus [1]–[370], every claim grounded in three aligned sources:
the Spanish Autograph (arbiter), the 1914 Mullan English translation, and a primary
Russian translation. 50 concepts and 17 method principles — from the foundation
(the beginning, the goal, indifference) through method (composition of place,
discernment of spirits) to the election and the crown: love expressed in deeds.
