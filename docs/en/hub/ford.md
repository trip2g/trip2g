# Henry Ford — My Life and Work

A knowledge base built from Henry Ford's **My Life and Work** (1922, with
Samuel Crowther; Project Gutenberg #7213).

Gathers Ford's philosophy of service-over-profit business ethics: business as
service, not a "bonanza"; fighting waste; high wages as sound calculation; cutting
prices as volume grows; standardization and the assembly line as method; the machine
as a liberator from want; money as a tool in the hands of the work, not its goal.
Every claim is grounded in a verbatim quote from the corpus.
