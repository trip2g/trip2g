# John D. Rockefeller — Random Reminiscences

A knowledge base on **Random Reminiscences of Men and Events** (1909), the
only book John D. Rockefeller (1839–1937) ever published — a short reflective
memoir in 7 chapters.

Gathers the business worldview of an organizer-capitalist: cooperation over ruinous
competition, patience and a long horizon, accounting discipline, honesty as
capital, trust in people, composure under attack — and the hard art of giving:
scientific philanthropy and the Benevolent Trust as an answer to the duty of great
wealth.
