# Napoleon Hill — The Law of Success

A knowledge base built on Napoleon Hill's **The Law of Success in Sixteen
Lessons** (1928, first edition; entered the public domain January 1, 2024). Every
claim is grounded in a verbatim quote from the corpus.

Covers the practical philosophy of success: definite purpose as the foundation,
organized effort, auto-suggestion and the subconscious as the mechanism of
achievement, the master mind alliance, persistence, faith, and transforming energy
into results. 25 concepts, 11 principles, 5 argument chains — all within the 1928
corpus.
