# Laozi — Tao Te Ching

A knowledge base of **Laozi**'s *Tao Te Ching* (道德經), all 81 chapters.
Built on verbatim quotes from Legge's English translation and the Chinese original
(ctext, Wang Bi), with working Russian glosses.

Answers questions about non-action (*wu wei*), naturalness (*ziran*), soft power and
yielding, measure and contentment, emptiness and simplicity, and rule without
interference — including Laozi's critique of Confucianism as a loss of the Tao. The
text is deliberately paradoxical; the base preserves the paradox rather than
flattening it into doctrine.
