# Wallace Wattles — The Science of Getting Rich

A knowledge base built on Wallace D. Wattles's **The Science of Getting
Rich** (1910) — the full corpus (497 units) with verbatim English quotes underlying
the entire system.

Unpacks Wattles's method: the formless substance from which all things are made,
the creative mind versus the competitive mind, the law of gratitude, holding a
definite image of what is desired, acting in the present, and giving an increase
— more than what is received.
