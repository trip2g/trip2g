# Pascal — Pensées

The anthropological-apologetic core of **Blaise Pascal**'s reasoning from his
major work **Pensées** (Brunschvicg numbering, sections I–VII). Every claim is
grounded in a verbatim quote from Trotter's translation.

A system of concepts on man between two abysses, diversion as flight from oneself,
heart against reason, the hidden God, and the wager as a decision under uncertainty.
Main line of argument: from nothingness to the wager, from the wager through habit
to faith.
