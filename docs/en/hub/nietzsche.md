# Nietzsche — Beyond Good and Evil

A knowledge base of late-period Nietzsche, extracted from aphorisms 1–296
and the preface of **Beyond Good and Evil** (1886). All claims are grounded in
verbatim quotes with DE/EN (Zimmern) / RU (Polilov) alignment.

Covers the will to power and the critique of self-preservation, perspectivism, the
genealogy of morals (master and slave), the critique of pity, the discipline of
suffering, and the eternal return. 27 concepts, 11 principles, 5 argument chains.
