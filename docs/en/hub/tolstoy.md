# Leo Tolstoy — Confession

A knowledge base of Leo Tolstoy's late-period worldview, extracted from his
philosophical treatise **Confession** (*Ispoved'*, 1879–1882, Russian Complete
Works vol. 23). Covers the crisis of faith, the testing of reason, spiritual
conversion, and the break with the church.

Structured into 36 concepts, 16 principles across 5 domains, and 4 argument
chains. Every claim is grounded in a verbatim quote from the corpus with chapter
references.
