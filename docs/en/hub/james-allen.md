# James Allen — As a Man Thinketh

A knowledge base built on James Allen's (1864–1912) primary text **As a Man
Thinketh** (1903, Authorized Edition; Project Gutenberg #4507) — the cornerstone
essay on the power of thought, unfolding one law across seven chapters: thought →
character → circumstance → body → destiny.

7 concepts covering the link between thinking and life conditions and destiny, 7
principles for recall and debate, and 3 chapter-internal argument chains. Every
claim is grounded in a verbatim quote; English is the primary source pool.
