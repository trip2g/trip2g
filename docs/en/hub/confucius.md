# Confucius — The Analects

A knowledge base of Confucius's worldview as a teacher of order, ritual, and
self-cultivation, extracted from **The Analects** (*Lunyu*, 20 books) — Chinese
original plus James Legge's verbatim English translation.

Covers self-cultivation and mastery over the self; core virtues (benevolence *ren*,
ritual *li*, rightness *yi*, virtue *de*); the noble man *junzi* versus the petty man
*xiaoren*; filial piety, loyalty, and reciprocity in relationships; learning and
knowledge; the link between speech and action; ritual and music as means of
cultivation; rule by example and the rectification of names; the Mandate of Heaven.
