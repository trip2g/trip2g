# Montaigne — Essays

A knowledge base on the philosophy of the skeptic Michel de Montaigne
(1533–1592) and his major work, the **Essays** (*Essais*). Gathers key concepts,
principles, and argument arcs from 11 central essays, "To the Reader," and a
limited slice of the "Apology for Raymond Sebond."

Answers the question: how to live fittingly when reason wavers, custom tyrannizes,
and the soul refuses to cohere into a system? Montaigne's weapon is the question
(*Que sais-je?*), the self-portrait, and the example — not a thesis.
