Publish your Obsidian vault as a website in under a minute.

Write in Obsidian, press Sync, your notes are live. The same hub also publishes to Telegram, gates paid content, and answers reader questions over MCP. A Markdown Operating System: one note, served to a human as a page and to an agent as a syscall.

New here? Follow **[[en/user/getting-started|Get your first note live]]**: instance → sign in → install the plugin → sync. About a minute.

## What you can do

- **Publish from Obsidian.** Your vault stays on your machine; sync pushes notes live, one-way or two-way. [[en/user/two-way-sync|Two-way sync →]]
- **Publish to Telegram.** Write once in Obsidian, post to your channel on a schedule, links stay intact. [[en/user/telegram|Telegram →]]
- **Charge for content.** Leave `free: true` off and a note is subscriber-only; take payment via crypto, Patreon, or Boosty. [[en/user/monetization|Monetization →]]
- **An AI that answers from your notes.** Connect any MCP client and readers query your knowledge base directly. [[en/user/mcp|MCP →]]
- **Make it look how you want.** Compose pages from frontmatter widgets, or drop in your own Jet templates. [[en/user/templates|Templates →]]
- **Run it yourself.** One Go process on SQLite; `docker compose up` on a laptop, a VM, or Fly. [[en/user/selfhosted|Self-hosting →]]

## Who uses it

Writers with paid channels, teams with internal wikis, course authors, open-source maintainers. [[en/user/use-cases|See the use cases →]]
