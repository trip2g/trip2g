You describe **what** you want, not **how** to get it. The system figures out the rest.

## Thermostat

There's a thermostat on the wall at home. You set it to 22°C — and that's it. You don't think about when to fire up the boiler, when to shut it off, or how to account for the weather outside. The system maintains the temperature on its own.

## Climate Control

In older cars, the heater and air conditioner are separate. Cold? Turn up the heat. Hot? Switch on the A/C. You monitor the temperature yourself and switch manually.

In modern cars there's climate control. Set it to 22°C and forget about it. The system decides on its own: heat, cool, adjust the fan speed.

trip2g is climate control for publishing.

## What It Looks Like

You write a note. You sync. It gets published to your channel.

Forgot an image — add it to the note, sync again. The image appears.

Changed the image — sync. It updates.

Fixed the text — sync. It updates.

You don't need to remember what's published where. You paint the picture — reality adjusts.

This is made possible by the [[en/thoughts/contentless-cms|Contentless CMS]] approach — your content lives with you, the platform just publishes it.
