A book's table of contents is the same for everyone. The author decided: first chapter 1, then chapter 2, then chapter 3. Identical for every reader, regardless of their experience or goals.

A table of contents is a single slice. Like folders on a computer — useful, but it shows the material from only one angle. Need a different perspective? Reorganize everything by hand.

People are different. The same material reads differently depending on the order. A beginner needs fundamentals → examples → practice. Someone experienced wants the nuances straight away. Someone in crisis needs a specific exercise right now.

---

## Entry Questionnaire

A Telegram bot asks a few questions. Who you are, what you know, why you're here. Based on the answers — it finds your entry point.

Not "here are 200 notes, good luck." But "here's where to start, and here's why."

A person takes a short assessment — and gets a link to a note matched to their situation. Not an abstract homepage, but a specific starting point.

---

## AI Builds a Personalized Table of Contents

AI reads the responses and assembles a route of 10–15 notes in the right order — tailored to that specific person. Not "all notes alphabetically," but a path with logic.

A psychologist wrote 100 notes. A new mother with postpartum anxiety gets one selection. An entrepreneur with insomnia gets another. A student before exams gets a third. The notes overlap, but the order and emphasis are different.

The author wrote the content once. AI packages it fresh for each reader.

---

## Four Ways to Navigate a Knowledge Base

A regular book offers one way: read in order. A knowledge base in trip2g offers four.

**Free exploration.** The reader moves through the [[en/thoughts/knowledge-graph|knowledge graph]] on their own. Opens a note, follows a link, dives deeper. Like Wikipedia — for people who like to explore.

**Curated track.** The author drew a route in Canvas. The [[en/thoughts/track-bot|bot in Telegram]] guides them through it step by step. For people who need structure.

**Personalized table of contents.** AI reads the questionnaire and assembles a route for that specific person. For those who don't fit into preset tracks.

**Question to the text.** The reader asks directly — AI answers from the author's knowledge base. Via [[en/thoughts/content-infrastructure|MCP]] or through the bot. Not a keyword search, but a conversation with an expert's knowledge.

One base. Four ways to move through it. Each reader chooses their own.

---

## What This Gives the Author

The author writes content once. The system guides readers on its own. A new type of audience emerged — add a track or adjust the questionnaire. No need to rewrite the base.

The content is stable. The ways to reach it — multiply.
