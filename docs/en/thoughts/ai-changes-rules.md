AI is the single biggest shift making knowledge graphs relevant right now.

### Readers Talk to Your Knowledge

Before, readers found information on their own: scrolling pages, following links, assembling a picture from fragments.

Now a reader asks an AI a question. The AI searches your knowledge graph, pulls context from linked notes, and formulates an answer.

A graph gives AI something linear text can't — the connections between concepts. AI understands that "diversification" is linked to "risk" and "asset correlation." A linear document doesn't convey that.

### The Expert Stops Repeating Themselves

A consultant answers the same questions dozens of times. It's exhausting. Over time, it leads to burnout.

AI takes on that work. Thousands of readers get a consultation simultaneously. The expert answers once — in a note. AI delivers that answer to everyone who asks.

### Creating Through AI, Reading Through AI

The new normal: authors write notes with the help of AI; readers consume them through AI.

An author dictates thoughts, AI structures them. An author sketches out theses, AI expands them. The author reviews and edits — but the heavy lifting is done by the machine.

A reader doesn't scroll pages — they ask a question. They get an answer tailored to what they asked, taking their context into account.

### Why This Changes Everything

Scaling expertise used to be impossible. One expert, a limited number of consultations per day.

Now expertise scales through a graph + AI. Knowledge is written once, available to everyone, answering questions 24/7.
