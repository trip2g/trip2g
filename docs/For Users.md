trip2g publishes your Obsidian vault as a website, a Telegram channel, and an AI assistant. You write markdown files in Obsidian; trip2g handles the rest. The site you are reading now is built only with trip2g.

[[ru/user/_index|_index]]

## Publish and earn

You publish notes and readers pay you directly. Each note becomes a page on your site, and wikilinks between notes resolve automatically. You mark each note as free or subscriber-only. Patreon and Boosty subscribers get access without extra setup.

## Reach readers everywhere

trip2g posts your notes to a Telegram channel on the schedule you set. It also runs an AI bot that answers reader questions using only your published content.
