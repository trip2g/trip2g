trip2g fits anyone who writes in Obsidian and wants to publish, earn, or share — without moving their notes to a platform they don't control. The site you are reading now runs on trip2g.

## Content creators

A YouTuber's research, scripts, and source notes never reach the audience — they stay in folders. trip2g turns that material into a paid layer behind the public content. The video is the entry point; the knowledge base is the depth subscribers pay for. Update a note in Obsidian; the site updates instantly.

## Course authors

A course is one fixed path through a knowledge base, but the base always holds more. trip2g lets you build multiple courses over a shared set of notes. Update a concept in one place and every course that links to it reflects the change. Students who know the basics skip ahead; students who miss a term follow the link, read it, and return.

## Expert consultants

Consultants answer the same questions dozens of times. An AI bot connected to your note base answers those questions for you — drawing only from what you have written. You step in for complex cases. The knowledge base scales; you don't burn out.

## Communities and teams

A private wiki for community members stops knowledge from disappearing into chat history. New members read the base instead of asking veterans. Each member adds notes; the base grows. A company uses the same setup with granular access: each team sees its own section and the shared core; a new hire gets access to the right sections on day one.

## Freelancers and consultants (personal bot)

A Telegram bot backed by your notes replaces the one-size-fits-all "about me" page. A recruiter asks about experience and gets experience. A partner asks about current projects and gets current projects. The bot tracks unanswered questions and shows you which notes to write next — not by guessing, but from real questions real people asked.
